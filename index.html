Why other exams are more resilient
All leak-resilient exams share what NEET lacks: (1) CBT delivery — no physical paper to steal, (2) smaller verified center networks, (3) rolling or adaptive question banks. NEET-UG will shift to CBT from 2027 per Education Ministry announcement.
Methodology & Limitations
Data Sources: NTA official statistics, NCRB Annual Reports 2013–2024, Parliamentary Standing Committee Report Dec 2025, NMC Lok Sabha data Aug 2025, CBI investigation records, Supreme Court orders, verified news reports.

Limitations: (1) Individual candidate-level data is not publicly available from NTA — all analysis uses aggregate numbers. (2) Financial impact figures are calculated estimates, not official data. (3) "Investigation involvement" maps only CBI/police-confirmed cases, not allegations. (4) Correlation between variables does not imply causation. (5) Kota suicide data for 2025 is partial (through May).
Sources (verified): NTA official statistics · NCRB Annual Reports 2013–2024 · Parliamentary Standing Committee Report Dec 2025 · NMC Lok Sabha data Aug 2025 · Rajasthan SOG / CBI investigation records 2019, 2024, 2026 · Supreme Court orders AIPMT 2015 & NEET 2024 · Jaipur Police FIR NEET 2021 · The Print, NDTV, Indian Express, The Hindu, Economic Times (2024–2026)
Corrections: 2009 & 2011 reclassified — Vyapam PMT was MP state exam, not AIPMT. 2021 reclassified Minor — confirmed Jaipur arrests. 2023 Jharkhand trunk theft relocated to 2024 per CBI FIR date.
